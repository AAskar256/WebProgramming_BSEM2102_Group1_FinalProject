About Us

Learn more about Sierra Leone Football Agency and our mission

History

Since our establishment, Sierra Leone Football Agency has been making significant headway in developing football talent across the nation. We have successfully placed over 50 players in professional clubs both locally and internationally.

Our progressive approach to player development and strategic partnerships has positioned us as a leading force in West African football management.
